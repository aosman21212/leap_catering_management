Leap AI Catering Management System for Odoo

Event Catering Orders — meal packages, tiered pricing, staff, invoicing, CRM, customer portal and analytics, all inside Odoo 19.

By LeapAI

Everything a Catering Business Needs

Built as a native Odoo app — extends your existing CRM, Accounting and Employees, no duplicate data entry.

Meal Packages & Tiered Pricing

Build reusable packages grouped by course, with a meal type, menu PDF, food photo gallery, and per-head price tiers that quote automatically from guest count.

Catering Orders

One order per event — customer, venue, date, guests, packages and additional charges, with totals that recalculate live and a draft → confirmed → invoiced → done workflow.

Flexible Invoicing

Invoice an order as a single line or split by package and charge — your choice per order, generating a real Odoo Accounting invoice in one click.

Additional Items & Products

Add service items like transportation and cleaning directly on the order, and manage a standalone product catalog for anything else you sell.

Staff Assignment

Mark any employee as catering staff and assign the right people to each event to plan who works it — no separate HR system.

Event Checklists

Load a reusable checklist template onto an order, mark items To Do / Done / Cancelled, and track completion percentage at a glance.

Tags & Bulk Actions

Tag orders for quick filtering, cancel several draft orders at once with a recorded reason, or bulk-apply tags across many orders.

Terms & Attachments

Keep reusable Terms & Conditions templates and pull them onto any order, plus a one-click button to every file attached to the order.

CRM Integration

Capture event details on a lead and generate a linked catering order in one click — no details retyped, with a two-way link back to the opportunity.

Customer Portal

Customers sign in to view their orders, sort / filter / search them, download a PDF, and rate their experience once the event is done.

Customer Ratings

Email a rating request after the event and see the customer's score and written review right on the order.

Reports & Dashboard

Print meal packages, orders and order history; review order analysis with pivot and graph views filterable by date and customer; export to Excel natively.

Inside the App

Organized into a single app with a clear top bar: Dashboard, CRM shortcut, and the full Catering workspace.
